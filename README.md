**Vice Grid Cover Studio** turns your own photos into a GTA 6-style multi-panel cover poster, right in the browser — no uploads, no account, no network. Pick one of 7 layouts (Nine Grid 3 × 3, Poster Nine hero + eight, Mosaic big + six, Strip Four, Quad 2 × 2, Triptych, Duo), drop in one photo or several, crop each panel by dragging and zooming, shuffle photos across panels, pick one of 8 Vice City grades (Sunset, Neon Night, Pastel Vice, Noir, Golden Hour, Magenta Dusk, Cyan Chrome, Duotone), and write a halftone title block: a small-caps kicker, a bold caption in sunset-gradient Anton lettering with outline and shadow, and a Pacifico script sub-caption. Then export a share-ready PNG — 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080, one at a time or all three at once.

Everything is original: the synthwave sun, skyline and palm silhouettes, the halftone dots, the neon frame and the grain are all drawn by the app's own canvas code. No Rockstar logo, artwork, character or font anywhere. The three fonts (Anton, Archivo Black, Pacifico) are SIL Open Font License files shipped under `app/fonts/`. The two sample photos are image-model-made and free to use.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Load a photo (or try a sample, or mix both), pick a layout and grade, edit the title block, and hit **Export PNG** — or **Export all 3 sizes at once**.
