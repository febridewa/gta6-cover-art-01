**Neon Coast Cover Studio** turns your own photos into a GTA 6-style multi-panel cover poster, right in the browser — no uploads, no account, no network. Pick a layout (Poster: hero + three panels, Strip: three tall panels, Grid: 2 × 2), drop in one photo or several, crop each panel by dragging and zooming, pick a Vice City grade (Sunset, Neon Night, Pastel Vice, Noir), write a bold caption in sunset-gradient Anton lettering with a Pacifico script sub-caption, and export a share-ready PNG.

Everything is original: the sun, skyline and palm silhouettes, the halftone title block and the grain are all drawn by the app's own canvas code. No Rockstar logo, artwork, character or font anywhere. The two fonts (Anton, Pacifico) are SIL Open Font License files shipped under `app/fonts/`. The two sample photos are image-model-made and free to use.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Load a photo (or try a sample), pick a layout and grade, edit the caption, and hit **Export PNG** — 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080.
